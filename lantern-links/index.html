How to play

Light up the whole board.

  1. Tap a tile to turn it.Right-click or long-press turns it back.
  2. Carry the light from the glowing core to every lantern.
  3. Light every tile to finish.No loops, no loose ends.
  4. Beat par for three stars.You get 3 undos per level.

The arrows beside the level number change level. Next opens after a solve.
